# Individual Contributor License Agreement

**Status:** in force since 2026-10-05 (version 1).

## How to sign (not part of the agreement)

The first time you open a pull request on this repository, a bot asks you to sign. Read the
agreement below, then reply on that pull request with exactly this comment:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

That comment is your signature. It is recorded with your GitHub account, the pull request and the
date, it covers every later contribution as well, and the bot then marks your pull request as signed.

If your employer, rather than you, owns the work you contribute, your employer signs the
[Corporate Contributor License Agreement](CCLA.md) instead.

In plain terms: you keep the copyright in what you write, and you give the maintainers a licence to
use and distribute it, including the right to relicense it later. This summary is only a guide; the
agreement below is what applies.
