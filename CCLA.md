# Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Status:** in force since 2026-10-05 (version 1).

## When this applies, and how to sign (not part of the agreement)

Use this agreement when your employer, rather than you, owns the work you contribute. Someone with
authority to sign for the company completes it, including Schedule A, the list of employees who will
contribute. To arrange signing, contact a maintainer: Dean Prichard (@zard49) or Marconi Sim
(@ptigroup). Once the signed agreement is on file, a maintainer adds the listed employees to the CLA
bot's allowlist, and their pull requests stop asking for an individual signature. When the company
removes someone from Schedule A, tell a maintainer: they come off the allowlist, and any later
contribution of theirs needs their own individual signature (§4).

*Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Corporate Contributor License Agreement v2.0.*

This agreement is between `ai-agent-security` ("the Project") and the corporation identified below
("Corporation"). It covers Contributions made to the Project by employees of the Corporation who are
designated below, where the Corporation owns those Contributions.

**1. Definitions.** "Maintainers", "Contribution" and "Submit" have the meanings given in the
Individual Contributor License Agreement in `CLA.md`. "You" means the Corporation.

**2. Grants.** Subject to these terms, You grant the Maintainers the same copyright licence set out
in §2 of the ICLA in `CLA.md`, and the same patent licence set out in §3, in respect of Contributions
owned by You and Submitted by a Designated Employee. **The sublicensing right in §2 is the operative
clause**, for the same reason it is in the individual agreement.

**3. You are entitled to grant this.** You represent that You have the corporate authority to enter
this agreement, that each Designated Employee is authorised to Submit Contributions on Your behalf,
and that each Contribution owned by You is an original work.

**4. Designated Employees.** You may Submit Contributions only through the employees listed in
Schedule A. You agree to keep Schedule A current, and to notify the Maintainers promptly when an
employee should be added or removed. A Contribution Submitted by someone not on Schedule A is
treated as an individual Contribution under the ICLA.

**5. No obligations.** Contributions are provided "AS IS", without warranties or conditions of any
kind, except to the extent of the representations in §3. The Maintainers are under no obligation to
accept, merge, or retain any Contribution.

**6. Successors and accuracy.** §7 and §8 of the ICLA apply equally to this agreement.
